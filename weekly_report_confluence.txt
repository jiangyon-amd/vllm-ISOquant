h1. Weekly Report: MXFP4 Fused Rotation+Quantization Kernel Optimization

*Date:* April 7, 2026
*Hardware:* AMD MI355X (gfx950, CDNA4)
*Framework:* vLLM + Quark 0.11 + aiter

h2. Summary

Completed E2E validation and benchmarking of fused rotation+quantization kernels for MXFP4 inference. The kernel fuses online rotation, MXFP4 quantization, and (for MoE) sorted scale scatter into a single launch, eliminating separate matmul and multi-kernel overhead.

*Highlights:*
* (/) Correctness verified: text generation (3/3 QA), PPL 9.08 (vs RTN 9.59)
* (/) CUDAGraph (FULL_AND_PIECEWISE) enabled for all runs
* (/) Two-run average on same GPU for stability (<1% RTN variance)
* (/) At c=32, fused rotation matches RTN baseline — online rotation with near-zero overhead

h2. Key Findings

# *HIP MFMA fused kernel is correct and fastest* — 11.8μs, 38% faster than Gluon Triton (19.1μs), 30× faster than separated
# *At c=32, fused matches RTN baseline* — online rotation adds near-zero overhead while improving accuracy (PPL 9.59→9.08)
# *MoE fused is the primary optimization for MoE models* — recovers 1.5–6.8% TPOT vs separated; up to 8.8% throughput gain
# *Dense models also benefit consistently* — 3.1–3.4% throughput recovery from Gluon fused attention kernel
# *Attention-fused alone is insufficient for MoE* — MoE expert layers dominate; must fuse MoE path for meaningful gain
